Pallet name AMBER-30 does not exist in PDAS yet and will be created.
No automatic rollback. A step that has run stays run.
This does not reach the machines. The operator picks the material on each winder's panel.

When the plan is blocked

Blocked

This blend, count and tube already exist as 205-IL0-SD Blue. Change its limits instead.

PDAS refuses a second product with the same blend, count and tube, even if the first is retired. To change a target weight, edit the limits of the existing product.
