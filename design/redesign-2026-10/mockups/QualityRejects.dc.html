About these figures
A cone that failed inspection and a cone the scale marked out of range are counted separately, each cone once. Reason codes are the plant's numbers (tube, material); IFL has not named them yet, so they show as codes until an engineer names them. Percentages use cones that were checked. Times are plant time and a production day runs 06:00 to 06:00.
