## Summary in plain English

**Complete (dev copy only).** Cone weighing and out-of-limit flagging, reject history and trends, sack logging, the line-level sack ledger, the live dashboard and TV wall, 14 stations, shift handling, audit trail, health monitoring, the calibration statistics, the user and role screens, and all 18 report types including IFL's eight. No cloud dependency: zero external requests were seen. Figures in the new app match the old app and direct SQL.

**Partly done, and why.** Almost every "partly" is the same two reasons. (1) IFL has not signed off anything: every KPI definition is "awaiting", the eight reports print "Assumed until IFL confirms", and OQ 22-29 were never sent. (2) Nothing has run outside this PC: no plant, no real printer, no Excel, no real signed-in viewer or engineer, no physical TV. Two features are partly built by nature: product changes (we observe a winder's product from its cones; we cannot switch it, and the PDAS write path is on only for the local copy) and "AI" (statistics only, and it must not be called AI until IFL agrees).

**Blocked on IFL.** A live read-only login and host; whether our PC can reach the databases; formal reply on PDAS writes and the writer login; sack stock per machine (the data has no machine); approval of the KPI definitions and report layouts; cone weight basis; the 10 Jul - 5 Aug data and a longer history; Urdu and floor-device decisions; whether the PLC reads limits from PDAS; anything AI.

**The owner must do.** Review `/next` and decide the switch-over; supply the Manrope woff2; run the two rehearsal kits (below-rank RBAC login, EXECUTE-only PDAS login) so the roles and the write path are proven beyond a render token; send `IFL-OPEN-QUESTIONS.md` (the first three asks, then items 22-29 and 30-41, all drafted, none sent); copy `D:\sms-backups` to other hardware and set an unattended backup; check the server PDF route on the real host; restart or keep the dev API; push (only the owner pushes). Decide the owner-reserved wording (the "rejected" titles, "Assumed until IFL confirms") and whether Hassan's product-per-shift scenario needs a declared changeover record.

**Is anything required missing that we could build now?** No required feature is wholly missing. Buildable now without waiting on IFL: (1) Excel/PDF restyle to meet the "beautiful, with graphics" answer (explicitly deferred in the redesign; the PDF host check stays the owner's); (2) the New-app polish found live: Today does not state the acquisition lag, `?at=` without a trailing Z fails with "Couldn't load plant clock", the station product label on Today/Stations/Wall shows the line default while Board shows per-MaterialId products, and "data batch" still appears on Weight and the Reason sheet (FC B7-8); (3) Reports and Setup bodies on the new table components (D10); (4) the SAT protocol, change-control procedure, release notes, and a pass over the manuals so they describe `/next`; (5) re-triage of the `DEFECTS.md` open-items index (D-1, R-17) so "no critical or high defects" can be quoted; (6) a declared per-machine changeover record, only if the owner reverses the 7 Oct decision. Do not build: per-machine sack stock, PLC links, AI/ML, or anything that guesses past an IFL answer (roadmap rule 17).
